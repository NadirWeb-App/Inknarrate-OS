Free text to speech that stays on your computer

Inknarrate reads your text aloud with natural voices that run inside your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded, there is no sign-up, and after your first visit it works offline. Add the voice models you like (Kitten TTS, Kokoro or Piper), pick English, Spanish or Arabic, and export the result as MP3 or WAV, with subtitles if you want them.

New here? Read the guide, or press Models and follow the quick start.
